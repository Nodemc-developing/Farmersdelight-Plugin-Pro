The thirty-two jug_legacy models combine these attributed shell meshes with this project's generated liquid volumes for clients before 1.21.4. The original shell geometry and textures retain the MIT license and vectorwing attribution; the generated liquid definitions are AGPL-3.0-only.
jug_legacy_liquid.png is this project's original fixed blue wave tile, generated without reading or copying external pixels and distributed under AGPL-3.0-only. Modern models retain their dynamic tint definitions.
